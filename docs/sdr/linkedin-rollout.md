# LinkedIn construction channel: offline release receipt

Date: 2026-10-19. Scope: company `13105180`, one server-bound Unipile v1 LinkedIn Classic (`ACo`) account. This is an offline software build. The migration has been applied only to disposable local PostgreSQL test schemas, never production. No provider connection, hosted link, invitation, DM, CRM note, or other external write was made. The sender account, credentials, real profile matches, payloads, and native conversation links remain unverified.

The channel defaults to disabled. An account has `mode='disabled'`, `read_budget=0`, and no verified read timezone; campaigns default unapproved with zero action budgets. Even after a mode is separately configured, the server requires the exact company/account binding, a verified Classic sender, an approved campaign digest, current CRM/owner/customer/Apollo/history evidence, an explicit account read allowance/timezone, and a separate `SDR_LINKEDIN_ALLOW_WRITES=true` flag before any provider POST. Hosted reconnection has its own disabled-by-default gate. None of these gates is enabled by this build.

## Offline review path

The private [offline review CLI](../../scripts/sdr-linkedin-offline-review.md) accepts a dated, independently referenced source/profile/CRM/project/email-completion/reply-review packet. It supports staged-candidate evidence refresh, an unapproved campaign revision, assignment, and a held dry-run preview without provider requests or action rows. Files are local and output is exclusively created with `0600` permissions. Browser-supplied eligibility and copy facts remain rejected. The packet records reviewed evidence; it is not proof of fresh connected eligibility or a real AGC/LBA cohort. No real candidate profiles were inferred from names or CRM notes.

The approved-campaign validator pins company, account, Classic sender, source/cohort, owner, copy, weekday 08:00–17:00 window, increasing business-day waits, budgets, stop policy, and expiry in the digest. Invitation and follow-up due times move into that window; DM1 remains event-driven. Dispatch rechecks the current campaign, account, candidate, owner mapping, holds, email reply, exact verified link, timing, and action fence after fresh asynchronous reads and before durable `network_started`. A changed local authorization or a send-window/acceptance-day transition holds the action before POST. Outstanding invitations reserve possible DM1 capacity. Unknown provider results stay uncertain and cannot be retried by a worker restart.

The adapter charges every GET/page to one atomically updated, sender-local daily account request budget before transport, including failed and partial requests. With the default zero allowance, it makes no read. Fresh eligibility and inbound verification use one page per list and hold on incomplete coverage; fresh chat fanout is capped. Recovery records a daily jittered slot before scanning at most one pending-invitation page and one relation page, so partial/failing scans cannot repeat each minute. Account restrictions cannot be overwritten by an unresolved-event warning.

Inbound webhooks are authenticated and account-bound. A mapped inbound or unknown-direction event commits the person hold, pending-action cancellation, event-person linkage, and email suppression before any fallible provider/history/draft work. Verified multi-project links suppress all mapped email addresses without guessing project attribution. Exact own outbound receipts avoid staff-takeover classification; early echoes stay unresolved until receipt correlation. Unmatched staff outbound stops the sequence. Private generic acknowledgment drafts require an exact inbound message, verified chat, current CRM owner, app-to-Pipedrive owner route, and a final candidate revision check. Draft access follows the current owner while preserving intended staff-author access and edits. The acknowledgment is unsent and makes no price or bid-scope claim.

Optional CRM handoff has a durable draft/revision/preview intent before a single publication boundary. Concurrent calls share that intent, uncertain outcomes cannot replay, and reconciliation requires the exact operation marker. Publication remains disabled without a separate native-link and authorization gate; no real publisher is wired. Reporting excludes test or historical deals unless a separate reviewed channel-attribution observation and event chronology support an outcome. Unavailable outcomes remain unavailable rather than being shown as zero.

```sh

Full repository/build results belong in the final integration review after all concurrent changes are frozen. Pilot readiness then requires explicit approval of copy, owner routing, limits and individual live actions, followed by readback of actual IDs and a real inbound stop. The prior read-only account inventory attempt was blocked by the local outbound-action guard; do not bypass it. Keep production migration unapplied and all modes disabled until those gates are reviewed.
